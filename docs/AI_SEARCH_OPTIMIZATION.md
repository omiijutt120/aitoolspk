## Current Google eligibility and the 2026 update

A supporting page needs to be indexed and eligible for a Search snippet. Google's newer [generative AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) (updated 10 July 2026) also requires site inclusion in Search generative AI features. Meeting requirements does not guarantee selection.

After verification, the owner should inspect **Settings > Search generative AI** and record the effective choice, including inheritance. Google's [control documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024) states worldwide rollout on 31 August 2026; inclusion is the default, and child properties may inherit their parent's choice. Exclusion affects links and grounding in the covered Search AI experiences. Default behavior is not evidence of this property's actual state.

Documentation discrepancy: the older [AI features page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), updated 10 December 2025, says no additional requirements and describes combined Web reporting. Use the newer guide and specific control/report help pages for today's setting and reporting details. ## Useful work for this catalog

Treat these as proposals for owner-reviewed content, not claims that all offers are approved:

- Answer the buyer's immediate question in visible text: what the plan includes, PKR price, duration, access type, relevant limits, and how to confirm current availability.
- Explain Pakistan-specific ordering and support with the actual business process. Distinguish verified facts from facts requiring confirmation on WhatsApp.
- Make category and brand comparisons useful through meaningful differences and limitations. Avoid creating many near-identical city, keyword, or plan pages.
- Cite official vendor documentation for capabilities and terms. Give a genuine review date only after reviewing; identify the responsible business or author where appropriate.
- Show delivery method, renewal, refund, and eligibility conditions accurately. Do not imply vendor authorization or guaranteed access without evidence.

These are applications of Google's emphasis on original information, clear sourcing, reliable authorship, and reader benefit. Prefer firsthand, verifiable explanations to rewriting vendor descriptions. [Google: helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

Keep important content in crawlable HTML text with descriptive native links between homepage, categories, collections, and details. Verify correct successful responses and mobile usability. Maintain query-copy canonicals and distinct pagination. Google Search still needs accessible, working pages with indexable content. [Google: technical requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical)

Preserve intentional `noindex` on offers awaiting resale/delivery approval. Those pages are not AI-search targets while excluded from indexing. Fix eligibility facts before considering an indexing change; increased URL count is not a success metric.

## Avoid unsupported shortcuts

Google's current guide says no special AI schema, Markdown version, or `llms.txt` is necessary; Google Search ignores `llms.txt`. It rejects mandatory content chunking or special AI phrasing. Organize for readers and accessibility. Do not buy artificial mentions, stuff keywords, publish mass-generated low-value pages, or promise AI placement. [Google: optimization guidance and mythbusting](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

Only mark up truthful visible content. Schema validity cannot guarantee rich results or AI inclusion. Product and merchant eligibility require separate review; WhatsApp inquiries do not prove an on-site purchase flow. [Google: product types](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product), [structured data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies)

## Crawling, previews, and training are separate choices

Googlebot access governs Search crawling. Page-level `noindex`, `nosnippet`, `max-snippet`, and text-level `data-nosnippet` control indexing/previews in different ways; do not remove intentional controls just to seek visibility. [Google: robots directives](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots-meta-tag)

The Search generative AI control is not an AI-training control. Its current help page points to Google-Extended for training choices. The [common crawler reference](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers) describes Google-Extended for Gemini training/grounding and says it does not affect Search inclusion/ranking. Those scope descriptions differ; do not infer that one robots rule controls every Google AI use. Recheck the exact official product guidance before changing crawler policy. [Google: Search AI control scope](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024)

This work adds no tracker or crawler-policy change and exposes no private customer information.
