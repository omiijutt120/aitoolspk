## Schema and indexing boundary

All **817 local offers** remain commercially pending: original 811 plus six new variants. Their detail URLs remain crawlable, self-canonical and **noindex**, omitted from the sitemap, with no Product/Offer JSON-LD. This is intentional. An in-stock badge or owner-authored price does not establish authorization to resell. Indexable homepage, categories, collections, pagination and editorial routes provide useful comparison destinations independently.

The private approval gate requires documented authorization, safe delivery, review/stock dates and exact public plan/access/warranty/renewal terms. Risky original listings also require resolution of flagged claims. Evidence is never copied to the public catalog. Renew quoted prices only after actually confirming them; do not reset all dates during builds. The original 19 October quotes expire under the existing seven-day rule on their actual schedule.

Local JSON parsing, export consistency and browser assertions are **not an official Google Rich Results Test or Schema.org validation result**. External tools cannot validate these unpublished URLs. After an authorized release, the owner should test representative public eligible Article/Breadcrumb pages and any genuinely approved product page in [Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) and [Schema.org Validator](https://validator.schema.org/). Product snippet and merchant listing eligibility must be reviewed separately; WhatsApp enquiries do not guarantee merchant eligibility or rich-result display.
