# Competitor intelligence: Pakistan subscription discovery

Research snapshot: **23 October 2026**. Target: **https://aitoolspk.tech/**. This is a bounded qualitative refresh, not a Google ranking report or a market-wide price survey. Earlier same-day evidence remains in [the original competitor report](../COMPETITOR_SEO_RESEARCH.md); observations below were refreshed for this assignment unless explicitly marked historical.

## Method, actual tools and coverage

Used the installed **competitor-profiling** workflow: inspect public positioning and plan pages, separate observation from inference, preserve dated source evidence, and leave unavailable quantitative fields empty. Used **content-strategy** for the information-gain gate and parent/topic planning, **link-building** for earned-link qualification, and **public-relations** for the separate PR plan. Read each skill's Codex adapter and the installation's runtime compatibility and AiToolsPk project context. These are installed Agent Skills, not authenticated SEO databases.

Executed ordinary `web.run` searches in six batches, followed by four batches of public text opens. Evidence: `reports/seo/research-observations.json` contains **24 observations across 16 existing collection slugs**; ten original tool-response snapshots are retained in ignored `reports/seo/research-raw/`. The observations associate results with discovery query families because batched search output does not establish a URL's position for every exact query. Raw files may be regenerated or discarded without changing public catalog data.

No DataForSEO, Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Search Console or analytics account data was available to this bounded task. No controlled Pakistan Google desktop/mobile capture was available. **Rank, search volume, CPC, numerical difficulty, traffic, referring domains, backlinks, customer counts and conversion performance remain unavailable.** Ordinary search appearance is not a position or demand measurement. Competitor mobile rendering, performance and structured-data validity were not tested; text extraction can omit scripts and interactive content.

Publisher discovery adds a separate information competitor: [TechJuice's Pakistan Gemini student story](https://www.techjuice.pk/google-launches-gemini-student-hub-with-new-ai-study-tools-in-pakistan/) addresses education availability. It is a potential editorial audience, not a confirmed prospect, backlink or ranking competitor for every collection. The Express Tribune story used in the PR plan was visible in search; its direct open returned HTTP 403, so only topic relevance from the publisher's search extract is recorded.

Official vendor pages are another competing answer type. Their pricing and documentation answer entitlement questions, while local sellers answer access and PKR purchasing questions. AiToolsPk should explain the distinction rather than imply that local variants are identical official packages. ## Changes and non-findings

The earlier report could not open PakTools pages; this refresh opened its guide hub and obtained the Lovable product extract. That improves research coverage; it does not establish that site availability improved. The Iku Figma extract changed between search and open, illustrating why variant counts and stock must be dated. No competitor speed improvement, ranking movement or backlink acquisition was established.

The earlier [two-domain evidence](../DOMAIN_AND_VENDOR_RESEARCH.md) remains historical to this refresh: `aitoolspak.tech` and `aitoolspk.tech` showed overlapping subject matter and the same public WhatsApp number. No ownership, migration permission, shared legal identity or SEO penalty is inferred. Do not redirect or change the older site from this research.

## Repeatable monitoring

Monthly, or after a material plan change, rerun a small representative query set and open source URLs; write a new dated observation set rather than silently replace history. Record locale/device/tool capabilities, result URL and text evidence separately. Only add measured ranks, volumes, page-level referring domains or field performance when an authorized source supplies them. Before publishing a comparison, recheck volatile source facts and the actual offer's private commercial gate. Monthly research is a recommended owner-run cadence, not an installed background job.
