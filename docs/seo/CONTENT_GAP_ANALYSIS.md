## Selection standard

The useful difference is a clearer, verifiable purchasing decision: exact duration, actual access, warranty source, allowance and price freshness. Catalog size is not evidence of expertise. Official brand documentation can explain a workflow, but only reviewed offer data can establish what a package actually supplies. Do not fill missing product facts from another seller's page.

## Guides worth improving now

The existing **choosing subscriptions** guide can add a reusable task → required features → ownership → total PKR → warranty worksheet. The existing **credits and duration** guide can explain the distinction between allowance period, access term and seller support period with a neutral worked example. Both improvements add decision value without claiming hands-on product testing or supplier entitlement.

Before a new article, write one sentence specifying its information gain and check whether the existing collection/guide already answers it. A separate app-versus-API guide may be justified if it provides a provider-specific decision tree with current official billing sources. A generic list of the same tools, a second brand-price landing page or a city name substituted into the same copy is not justified.

## Per-offer review, not blanket indexing

For every catalog ID, the product intelligence system should retain its parent, missing facts, content differentiation and recommended next action. Parent-level research is explicitly not individual-offer verification. Even a low-price or in-stock variant stays under the existing noindex/schema/sitemap gate until commercial eligibility and useful differentiated content are established. A verbal intention to send delivery evidence is not the evidence itself.

Only approved public fields may feed a buyer-facing comparison. Keep private proof and confidential supplier material outside public data, reports, client bundles and Git history. Search facts or competitor claims never manufacture commercial approval.

## Validate the gap after publication

Track actual questions in authorized owner-supplied Search Console exports, site search and consented support notes; do not collect customers' credentials. A WhatsApp click records enquiry intent, not a sale. Use repeated misunderstanding to improve the relevant parent/guide before adding URLs. Recheck volatile vendor facts and price dates at review time. No traffic uplift, conversion uplift or complete market gap was measured in this work.
