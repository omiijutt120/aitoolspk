# Digital PR and earned discovery plan

Prepared **23 October 2026** using the installed **link-building** and **public-relations** workflows and their Codex compatibility rules. This is a preparation document. **No outreach, messages, account creation, listing submission, paid placement or background campaign has been executed.**

## Establish what is worth citing

There is no measured backlink dataset, referring-domain comparison, AI-citation dataset or Search Console Links export in this research task. We cannot diagnose an authority bottleneck, claim a link gap or say that any outlet cites/recommends AiToolsPk. First make the intended destination useful, reachable and eligible; paid promotion cannot fix an unapproved commercial offer.

Recommended assets, in order:

1. An openly accessible subscription comparison worksheet: task, access ownership, term, warranty, allowance, total PKR and quote date. Link existing guides and collections. A complete worksheet is more useful than a claim to be the cheapest.
2. A transparent catalog-methodology note explaining grouped variants, exact public IDs, price freshness and the commercial-review gate. Catalog statistics must be calculated from public sanitized data and labeled as AiToolsPk's catalog, never Pakistan market share or a representative price index.
3. A short, sourced app-versus-API decision guide, with actual billing-owner and secret-handling questions. Use official provider documentation, with no credential collection or workarounds.
4. An original test report only after performing and documenting the tests, accounts, workload, date and limitations. Do not claim hands-on usage, outcomes, national survey results or user testimonials that do not exist.

## Recommended tactics

Begin with an asset and relevant audience. Reclaim a brand mention only after finding the actual mention. Propose a useful resource only where the site's stated scope fits. Offer an expert explanation only with an identifiable authorized spokesperson and defensible facts. A commercial directory is worth considering only if it accepts this type of comparison/reseller business and disclosure is accurate. Do not mass-submit an AI subscription catalog as though it developed the underlying tools.

The older `aitoolspak.tech` site is not a link asset we can control. Do not request redirects, deindexing, canonical changes or link replacement without actual ownership/access and authorization. Shared WhatsApp details alone establish none of those permissions.

## Risk controls and measurement

No paid links intended to pass ranking credit, private blog networks, bulk reciprocal schemes, fake reviews, false expert bylines, automated posting, guaranteed placements or unauthorized email scraping. Sponsored or compensated placements require clear disclosure and appropriate `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`; a free product supplied for a linked review is compensation. Use natural branded/descriptive citations, not mandated keyword anchors. See [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).

Owner-run monthly review: log the actual prospect URL, qualification evidence, permission/scope, message date if subsequently authorized, reply, editorial coverage, actual link target/attribute and referral/enquiry evidence. Unknown values stay unavailable. Count a placement only after checking the live page. An AI citation is measured only with a dated prompt, engine/interface and actual cited URL; ordinary search appearance is not AI visibility.

At 30 days, aim to finish a useful asset and establish the measurement baseline. At 60 days, review any owner-authorized outreach and editorial feedback. At 90 days, compare actual eligible-page discovery and relevant enquiries with the baseline. These are review milestones, not promised link, rank, citation or sales gains.
