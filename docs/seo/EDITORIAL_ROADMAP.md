# Evidence-led editorial roadmap

Prepared **23 October 2026**. The installed **content-strategy** workflow informed the information-gain gate, hub/spoke mapping and distribution planning. All dates below are proposed review windows, not publication commitments or an installed recurring job. No article, post, outreach message or account integration is published by this document.

## Purpose and evidence levels

Help Pakistani buyers understand task fit, actual access, allowance, duration, warranty and total PKR cost before a WhatsApp enquiry. Improve the existing 12 priority collection explanations and two buying guides before adding a large article inventory. [Keyword strategy](KEYWORD_STRATEGY.md) keeps broad buying intent on existing parent URLs. [Competitor intelligence](COMPETITOR_INTELLIGENCE.md) supplies observed topic overlap; it does not establish measured search demand.

Four editorial pillars: selecting a suitable workflow; understanding access and commercial terms; managing credits/duration/budget; and understanding developer/API ownership. Urdu or Roman Urdu is a later audience hypothesis requiring real query evidence and a competent editor; do not mass-translate current English pages.

## Information architecture and editorial acceptance

Each new proposal must state the distinct user task, primary destination, one-sentence information gain, sources, missing evidence, commercial gate and internal-link plan. If it repeats a parent or the existing two guides, merge the useful material there. Do not create every combination of brand, city, duration, warranty and year.

Use clear headings and concise direct answers supported by visible tables, examples or checklists when helpful. Source vendor facts near the claim, distinguish brand functionality from package entitlement, and show the actual review date. Publish no invented author identity, customer quote, rating, address, test result or publication date. Add Article markup only when it truthfully describes the final page and date history; do not add retired FAQ rich-result claims.

All product links must remain functional and retain exact IDs, real prices and duration/warranty distinctions. Pending offers retain noindex/sitemap/schema restrictions. An editorial guide can explain how to evaluate an offer without certifying it. Quotes must obey freshness rules; source facts must not silently change catalog price or entitlement data.

## Distribution preparation

For each useful guide, prepare two owner-reviewable derivatives: a short checklist for the relevant community and a factual summary suitable for a qualified educational publisher. Keep commercial interest visible. Do not post, message, upload, submit listings or collect email addresses as part of this plan. No signup, cart, paywall or payment flow is introduced. [Digital PR strategy](DIGITAL_PR_STRATEGY.md) defines future qualification and unsent examples.

## Measurement and update cadence

At 30 days after authorized publication, check actual crawl/index status and query/page fit from owner-authorized Search Console evidence. At 60 days, refine the sections that generate confusion or relevant impressions without useful engagement. At 90 days, decide whether an additional guide is justified by actual questions, query evidence and workload capacity. An enquiry click is not a sale; distinguish enquiry quality, completed orders and attribution only when those records actually exist.

For every planned or released article, maintain owner/editor, state, actual source-check date, source URLs, canonical destination, related parents, commercial restrictions, observed query evidence, review trigger and next review date. Unknown metrics stay unavailable. No traffic/ranking/citation/lead uplift target is promised without a measured baseline. This is a quality-controlled backlog rather than a promise to publish all candidates.
