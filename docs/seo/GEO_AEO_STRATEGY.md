## Search eligibility comes first

Google's current guide treats AI search optimization as SEO: pages must be indexed and eligible for snippets, and the site must participate through its Search AI control. Helpful original information is the editorial target. Eligibility is not selection. [Google AI optimization](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)

After owner verification, inspect **Settings > Search generative AI** and record effective include/exclude/inherit, parent property and date. The documented default is not an account observation. This control is distinct from AI-training preferences; this assignment changes neither. [Search AI control](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024?hl=en)

The safest current targets are useful collections, categories and editorial guides. Offers lacking complete review retain crawlable noindex, sitemap exclusion and no Product/Offer. All offers still receive research and an explicit action record. Owner-confirmed stock for the original 811 and the newer generic eligibility reply do not supply reviewed delivery, authorization, claims and price freshness for every offer.

## Useful content, links and schema

Preserve the existing hierarchy: home → category → collection → offer. Link guides contextually to the relevant collection and policies; link collection guidance back to the most useful explanation. Use native links and readable server-rendered text. Keep API grouping last and preserve product variant selection and exact WhatsApp messages.

Use Organization/WebSite for actual identity, BreadcrumbList for real hierarchy, CollectionPage/ItemList for visible directory entries and Article for genuine editorial pages. Product markup needs separate offer review and visible matching facts. Broad brand lists and mixed-provider APIs are not one product. Google's documented ProductGroup variation properties do not currently include subscription duration or warranty; do not manufacture properties to force eligibility. [Product introduction](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product), [variant guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-variants)

Merchant/free-listing eligibility remains a separate assessment of the business model and supported product flow. No cart/payment flow is added merely to pursue markup. A syntactically valid JSON-LD document is not a completed Google Rich Results Test or evidence of guaranteed appearance. [Structured data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies)

Visible helpful questions remain appropriate, but FAQ rich results ended in May 2026. Do not add FAQPage for promised Google enhancement. [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)

## Work to avoid

No AI keyword density target, fixed answer length, fake citations, mass fan-out pages, artificial city pages or invented expertise. No mandatory llms.txt, special AI schema, duplicate Markdown version or bought mentions. An optional discovery file for another explicitly supported system must have a maintenance purpose; it is not a Google ranking requirement. [Current Google myth guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [scaled-content policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

Installed GEO/SEO scores can help prioritize reviewed evidence, but cannot establish an AI engine's ranking, citation probability or Google's internal metrics. Missing backlink, GSC or citation observations stay unknown. [Third-party tool evaluation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/third-party-seo)

## Measurement protocol

1. Owner verifies the property and records the Search AI control. Keep account evidence private.
2. Use the dedicated Search AI report when actually visible. It reports impressions by page/country/device/date; unavailable is not zero. Do not infer AI clicks, CTR or query-level performance from this report. Do not add its impressions to overall Web totals. [Google report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139?hl=en)
3. Record ordinary Web clicks/impressions separately, then review eligible landing pages and relevant query intent. Store date windows, country/device filters, time zone, row limitations and export date.
4. For an optional manual AI-answer sample, fix a small question set from the table above. Record actual platform/model, exact prompt, locale/device/account state, date, linked source URLs and screenshot or saved answer. Separate brand mention from cited link; record non-observations and failed tests. No platform was tested for citations in this document.
5. Repeat like-for-like monthly. Treat changed answers as sample observations, not population-wide share of voice. Evaluate business usefulness through aggregate enquiry quality; a link click cannot establish a sale.

For crawler-specific changes on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or other services, first re-open that provider's current official crawler documentation. Do not generalize Google's Search AI setting or a user-agent probe to every engine. No cross-platform access change is prescribed without that evidence.

The [30/60/90-day roadmap](SEO_30_60_90_DAY_ROADMAP.md) defines measurable follow-through. Publication, account setup, campaigns and the older domain remain owner actions; the current local work does not authorize them.
