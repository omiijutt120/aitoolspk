## Assignment rules

Use the existing parent collection as the primary target for a brand's related price, plan and buying intent. A product's stable public ID still receives its own analysis and next action. Repeated primary keyword candidates across subordinate offers are intentional consolidation opportunities, not a reason to invent unique demand. The generated master product report is the complete offer-level map; this document defines its editorial rationale.

All candidate phrases below are **research-supported topic opportunities or explicitly unvalidated wording hypotheses**, not confirmed Google queries with measured volume. The sample covered 16 parent slugs; remaining collections receive labeled hypotheses until researched. Unknown impressions, clicks, rank, volume, CPC, difficulty and conversions must remain null/unavailable. A competitor page's existence establishes relevant content, not demand.

Priorities describe fit, evidence, current usable destination and implementation effort. They are not a numerical opportunity score. P1 targets existing destinations with demonstrated topic overlap; P2 needs additional factual review or deeper audience evidence. Commercial eligibility can block product indexing even when a keyword is relevant.

## Parent versus offer versus guide

- A collection owns broad brand buying intent. Keep paginated plan discovery and self canonicals technically consistent; pagination is not an excuse to create duplicate introductions.
- A differentiated, legitimately approved offer may eventually answer its own specific term/entitlement question. Do not redirect or canonicalize every offer to its parent merely because titles share a brand. Keep current self canonicals and noindex gates unless a reviewed architecture change is justified.
- A guide owns a different user task: choosing access, understanding credits, comparing workflows or planning cost. Link to relevant collections and exact approved offers where appropriate; it should not duplicate a price inventory.
- Avoid city-spun landing pages, automated Urdu translations, unlimited keyword combinations and changing URLs to chase every query. Validate genuine Urdu demand and editorial capability before adding a locale.

## Cannibalization review

Group all offers by the existing primary collection, normalize candidate phrase families and identify overlapping intent. Mark repeated brand intent as parent-targeted. Escalate actual duplicate content and redundant landing pages separately. Retain product IDs, prices and navigation even while pages are noindex. The older `aitoolspak.tech` domain remains an ownership/migration question, not an automatic canonical target or redirect authorization; see [domain research](../DOMAIN_AND_VENDOR_RESEARCH.md).

With authorized Search Console exports, compare actual page/query pairs by Pakistan, date range and device, noting average-position aggregation limits. Review whether overlapping URLs satisfy distinct tasks before consolidating anything. No Search Console query data was available to this research task.

## Acceptance and measurement

An assigned keyword must point to an existing canonical destination, have a stated evidence level and avoid unsupported entitlement claims. Each offer must still expose missing commercial evidence categories. Keep pending offers crawlable so search engines can read noindex, exclude them from sitemaps and purchase schema, and never treat stock confirmation as resale authorization. Official guidance: [noindex](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing).

After owner-authorized publication and account verification, establish actual impressions/clicks/CTR by destination, then enquiry intent separately from sales. Thirty-day actions: verify indexing and query fit. Sixty-day actions: improve answers from observed queries. Ninety-day actions: assess conversion quality and expand only justified content. No growth percentage, rank or lead target is claimed without a baseline.
