# Measurable 30/60/90-day SEO growth roadmap

Prepared **23 October 2026**. Day 0 starts after an **authorized release** and actual owner verification establish a usable baseline, not when this document was written. The current enterprise changes are local-only. Search impressions, traffic, rankings, completed enquiries/orders, backlinks and AI citations are not available as measured baselines. Targets below concern quality, evidence and execution; no ranking/traffic outcome is guaranteed.

Owners: **business owner** supplies commercial facts and controls accounts/publication; **maintainer** handles source, tests and reproducible technical defects; **editor/reviewer** checks factual content and sources. One person may perform several roles, but must retain the evidence boundary.

## Before Day 0: P0 prerequisites

| Action                                                           | Owner                       | Completion evidence                                                                     | Dependency / safe fallback                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Preserve prices, IDs, variants, design and real WhatsApp targets | Maintainer                  | Catalog integrity and browser/release reports                                           | Do not trade working enquiry flow for more indexed URLs                                 |
| Record every offer's analysis/publication state                  | Maintainer                  | Full-catalog master and coverage report with no omissions                               | Unknown performance stays null; eligibility is mandatory, separate from quality score   |
| Review missing commercial categories privately                   | Business owner              | Actual authorization/delivery/terms/claims review reflected by the established importer | Original stock confirmation and newer verbal confirmation do not fill unspecified facts |
| Prepare a reviewable release and rollback                        | Maintainer + business owner | Local checks and exact changes reviewed; explicit publication decision                  | No push/deploy until authorized; local reports are still useful                         |
| Verify Search Console ownership and sitemap                      | Business owner              | Actual account verification/submission dates and status                                 | Follow [owner setup](GOOGLE_SEARCH_CONSOLE_SETUP.md); do not invent TXT values          |

## Days 1–30: establish reliable discovery and evidence

| Workstream           | Concrete action                                                                                                                      | Measurable acceptance                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Release verification | Check HTTPS/apex/www, homepage, directory, category, collection, detail, policies, assets, no-JS navigation, pagination and WhatsApp | Every tested path has an artifact/status; unexpected failures assigned and resolved or explicitly blocked                                |
| Indexing             | Inspect at least six representative eligible/control URLs with the owner; record declared/selected canonical and expected noindex    | All inspected URLs classified; unexpected exclusion reasons have an owner and next check                                                 |
| Catalog pipeline     | Re-run product SEO analysis when sanitized catalog changes                                                                           | 100% public IDs represented exactly once; prices/IDs preserved; each record has a state and next action; no manufactured approvals       |
| Content              | Review the 12 priority collections and existing buying guides before adding URLs                                                     | Every chosen page has distinct buyer value, source support, useful limitations and contextual internal links                             |
| Performance          | Capture representative mobile/desktop lab runs; read actual field data if available                                                  | Reports record URL/revision/tool/device/window; field unavailable remains null; no TBT→INP substitution                                  |
| Measurement          | Owner exports available Web/AI reports or records unavailability                                                                     | Property/date/filter/row-grain metadata accompanies every export; an actual comparison baseline or explicit missing-data register exists |
| Trust and enquiry    | Manually inspect selected exact-plan messages and buyer-facing policies                                                              | Price, duration, warranty provider, public ID and confirmation intent match visible facts; no password/security-code collection          |

Google's Search Essentials do not guarantee indexing; expected held offers remain excluded. [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials)

## Days 31–60: test focused content and conversion hypotheses

Use two equal, complete 28-day windows with consistent filters when enough time/data exist. If not, delay the comparison and record why; do not manufacture a growth percentage. Review the top 10 relevant available queries/landing pages, or all if fewer. Available query data may omit private queries and lower-volume rows. [Performance dimensions](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011259?hl=en)

| Hypothesis                                             | Action                                                                                | Evidence to compare                                                               | Stop/review rule                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A clear parent comparison satisfies shared plan intent | Improve one existing collection's verified comparison and internal links              | Same page/query/device segments, actual useful enquiries if recorded              | No extra thin product URL merely to create a keyword target  |
| Buyers need clearer access/expiry facts                | Resolve actual package questions; update approved public terms and one relevant guide | Non-identifying enquiry categories and selected page behavior                     | Do not invent delivery/rights to complete the table          |
| Relevant high-impression pages have unclear titles     | Change a small reviewed title/description set only after checking intent and position | Before/after comparable GSC impressions/clicks/CTR plus change date               | Low data or confounding release changes mean inconclusive    |
| Slow templates impede use                              | Fix measured image/text/rendering/bundle bottleneck, rerun affected template          | Matching lab conditions; field window when available                              | No broad rewrite or loss of accessibility for a single score |
| One practical explanation deserves a dedicated page    | Produce one or two reviewed guides only when the gap is distinct                      | Sources, original decision table/examples, intended URL, related collection links | Merge into existing page if intent/value overlaps            |

Completion: three prioritized evidence-linked actions have an owner, change/test record and status. Unavailable data produces a research task, not a fabricated success. Digital PR activity remains draft/target research unless outreach/publication is separately authorized; never buy manipulative links or fake endorsements. [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

## Days 61–90: review outcomes and choose the next quarter

1. Reinspect the original URL/control sample; compare current canonical/indexing observations with the actual baseline.
2. Reconcile full-catalog analysis states after new reviews. Close every original blocker or defer it with a reason and owner; do not reward index-count growth that bypasses eligibility.
3. Review equal windows for relevant Web visibility, dedicated AI impressions if available, and aggregate enquiry quality. Separate branded/non-branded and country/device where the available data supports it.
4. Repeat the same small AI-answer observation panel only if real platform access and a reproducible protocol exist. Keep model, prompt, locale and cited URL records; no broad citation-share claim from a handful of answers.
5. Compare representative lab templates and actual field p75 LCP/INP/CLS when available. CrUX absence or origin-only data is a limit, not universal failure/pass. [CrUX methodology](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/crux/methodology)
6. Review the content experiment: keep useful accurate explanations, revise unsupported/outdated facts, consolidate overlapping intent, and select the next three priorities from observed problems.

Completion: a dated quarterly decision log reconciles every action, cites real artifacts, explains inconclusive experiments and sets the next review date. No target traffic, AI mention, top position or paid-order lift is asserted without observations.

## Old-domain decision remains conditional

The earlier public research found branding/contact overlap between `aitoolspak.tech` and `aitoolspk.tech`; it did not establish ownership, migration permission, rankings or backlinks. Keep the older site untouched. If the owner later proves control and approves migration, obtain both properties' verified ownership, map actual old URLs to relevant destinations, confirm a host capable of HTTP redirects, test permanent redirects and rollback, then follow Google's site-move/Change of Address requirements. Static Pages HTML is not an arbitrary server redirect engine. [Existing domain evidence](../DOMAIN_AND_VENDOR_RESEARCH.md), [Google Change of Address](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9370220?hl=en), [site moves](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes)
